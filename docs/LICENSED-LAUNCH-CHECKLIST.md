# Licensed real-money launch checklist

**Draft for review with legal counsel and the gaming regulator. This is not legal advice and does not certify the software.**

This checklist is for a future, separately licensed real-money operation. The
current E-Sabong Virtual Console remains a **play-money system**: credits have
no cash value and it has no cash-in, cash-out, payment gateway or physical-cash
settlement. A licence does not make the existing code compliant by itself.

Use this with a lawyer familiar with the **specific jurisdiction and venue**
where you intend to operate. Requirements differ by country, state/province,
city and, for cockfighting, by local permits and animal-welfare rules.

---

## 1. Jurisdiction and activity

- [ ] Confirm the exact jurisdiction, venue and activity to be licensed.
- [ ] Confirm that online/remote betting on cockfights is permitted there, and on what terms.
- [ ] Confirm whether the licence is national, regional, local or venue-specific.
- [ ] Confirm whether the licence covers remote players, on-premises players, or both.
- [ ] Confirm whether agents, franchises or third-party operators are allowed.
- [ ] Confirm the regulator's rules on live-streaming, fight recording and result evidence.
- [ ] Confirm local business permits, zoning, fire/safety and animal-welfare requirements.
- [ ] Confirm restrictions on advertising, sponsorships, and targeting vulnerable groups.
- [ ] Obtain a written legal opinion on the intended operating model.

## 2. Corporate and licensing

- [ ] Incorporate the operating entity in the required form and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Identify beneficial owners, directors and key personnel for suitability checks.
- [ ] Complete fit-and-proper / probity disclosures for owners and management.
- [ ] Appoint the required licensed persons (operator, compliance officer, AML officer).
- [ ] Pay licence fees and establish the required capital or bond.
- [ ] Confirm licence conditions, reporting deadlines and renewal dates.
- [ ] Register trademarks, domain names and payment/bank accounts in the licensed entity's name.
- [ ] Put contracts in place with any platform supplier, payment provider, security vendor and data processor.

## 3. Player onboarding, KYC and AML/CFT

- [ ] Implement identity verification before a player can deposit, bet or withdraw.
- [ ] Verify legal name, date of birth, address and a government-issued ID.
- [ ] Enforce the regulator's minimum age and block minors.
- [ ] Screen against sanctions, politically exposed persons and prohibited lists.
- [ ] Apply risk-based enhanced due diligence for high-value or unusual players.
- [ ] Implement source-of-funds and source-of-wealth checks where required.
- [ ] Maintain a single player account per verified identity; detect duplicates and syndicates.
- [ ] Record consent, verification evidence and retention periods.
- [ ] Define suspicious-transaction and suspicious-activity reporting to the financial intelligence unit.
- [ ] Define record-keeping and reporting duties, including currency-transaction thresholds.
- [ ] Train staff on AML/CFT, tipping-off rules and escalation.

## 4. Money, payments and physical cash

- [ ] Open regulated bank accounts and merchant accounts in the licensed entity's name.
- [ ] Use only regulator-approved deposit and withdrawal methods.
- [ ] Prevent third-party and anonymous funding; match payment instruments to the verified player.
- [ ] Implement deposit, withdrawal and loss limits, including player-selected limits.
- [ ] Implement cooling-off periods for limit increases.
- [ ] Define cash-handling procedures for cashiers: start-of-shift count, dual control, blind drops, end-of-shift reconciliation.
- [ ] Issue numbered receipts for every cash movement and keep duplicate copies.
- [ ] Separate player funds from operating funds to the extent required.
- [ ] Define cash-vault limits, secure transport and insurance.
- [ ] Define how physical cash is converted to credits and back, with audit evidence at each step.
- [ ] Reconcile cash, bank, payment-provider and player-ledger balances daily; investigate every variance.
- [ ] Define chargeback, refund and disputed-bet procedures.
- [ ] Confirm tax treatment: gaming tax, withholding, VAT/GST and player tax reporting.

## 5. Game integrity and fairness

- [ ] Document the betting rules, odds derivation, stop ratio, draw ratio and settlement rules in player-facing terms.
- [ ] Obtain independent certification of the betting engine, RNG (if used) and payout calculations.
- [ ] Define who may open, lock, suspend and settle a fight, with separation of duties.
- [ ] Record an immutable audit trail for odds changes, side locks, results, regrades and voids.
- [ ] Define how a wrong result is corrected, who approves it, and how players are notified.
- [ ] Define void/cancelled-fight rules and refund timing.
- [ ] Verify the live video/result feed and retain recordings for the regulator's retention period.
- [ ] Define controls against insider betting, court-siding and result manipulation.
- [ ] Provide a player complaints and dispute-resolution process with response deadlines.
- [ ] Make the regulator's dispute/escalation route visible to players.

## 6. Responsible gambling

- [ ] Publish a responsible-gambling policy approved by counsel.
- [ ] Provide self-exclusion, time-out and account-closure tools that take effect immediately.
- [ ] Provide visible session reminders, deposit/loss limits and reality checks.
- [ ] Train staff to recognise and respond to distress, intoxication and problem gambling.
- [ ] Remove credit, loans or informal lending to players; prohibit betting on credit.
- [ ] Provide signposting to independent support services.
- [ ] Define how self-excluded players are prevented from opening new accounts.
- [ ] Confirm advertising and promotional rules, including bonuses and inducements.

## 7. Platform, security and privacy

- [ ] Complete a security architecture review and threat model.
- [ ] Encrypt data in transit and at rest; manage keys and secrets formally.
- [ ] Enforce strong authentication for staff, least privilege and mandatory MFA for admin roles.
- [ ] Separate production, staging and test environments; no test data in production.
- [ ] Log and monitor admin actions, logins, cashier actions and configuration changes.
- [ ] Set retention, backup, restore and disaster-recovery requirements; test restores.
- [ ] Complete a data-protection impact assessment and publish a privacy notice.
- [ ] Define lawful basis, consent, player access/correction/deletion rights and retention periods.
- [ ] Complete penetration testing and remediate findings before go-live.
- [ ] Define incident response, breach notification and regulator notification duties.
- [ ] Confirm data-localisation or cross-border transfer restrictions.
- [ ] Conduct vendor due diligence and include audit/security obligations in contracts.

## 8. Reporting, records and supervision

- [ ] Agree the regulator's reporting format, frequency and submission channel.
- [ ] Report events, fights, odds, stakes, results, payouts, voids and regrades as required.
- [ ] Report players, deposits, withdrawals and suspicious activity as required.
- [ ] Retain records for the regulator's minimum period; make them retrievable on request.
- [ ] Provide regulator access to premises, systems, staff and records.
- [ ] Appoint an internal audit or independent testing function.
- [ ] Define board/management oversight, compliance committee and escalation paths.
- [ ] Maintain a regulatory-change log and compliance calendar.

## 9. Operations and staffing

- [ ] Write operating procedures for opening, running and closing an event day.
- [ ] Define cashier, Game Master, supervisor and compliance roles with segregation of duties.
- [ ] Screen and train all staff; keep training records.
- [ ] Define access removal on termination or role change.
- [ ] Define opening/closing checklists, shift handover and dual-control requirements.
- [ ] Define escalation for system outages, disputed results, cash shortages and security incidents.
- [ ] Run a full dry run with test players and a regulator/counsel walkthrough.
- [ ] Arrange independent audit before accepting real money.

## 10. Player terms and disclosures

- [ ] Publish terms and conditions, betting rules, payout rules and void rules.
- [ ] Clearly disclose the licensed operator, licence number and regulator.
- [ ] Clearly disclose that bets are real money, the risks, and the minimum age.
- [ ] Publish the complaints, dispute and responsible-gambling routes.
- [ ] Publish the privacy notice and cookie policy.
- [ ] Confirm all player-facing copy and translations with counsel.

## 11. Go-live evidence pack

Prepare a signed pack for counsel and the regulator containing:

- [ ] Licence and permits, with conditions mapped to controls.
- [ ] Corporate ownership and fit-and-proper submissions.
- [ ] AML/CFT policy, risk assessment and reporting procedures.
- [ ] KYC vendor contract and test results.
- [ ] Payment and cash-handling procedures, with reconciliation evidence.
- [ ] Game-rules document and independent fairness certification.
- [ ] Responsible-gambling policy and self-exclusion test evidence.
- [ ] Security review, penetration-test report and remediation sign-off.
- [ ] Privacy impact assessment and data-processing agreements.
- [ ] Staff training records and role matrix.
- [ ] Incident-response and disaster-recovery test results.
- [ ] Regulatory reporting samples and retention schedule.
- [ ] Dry-run results, known issues and go/no-go sign-off.

## Gap assessment: current console vs a licensed cash operation

The existing software is a **play-money operations console**. The following
would have to be designed, built, independently tested and approved before it
could handle real money — they are not configuration changes.

| Requirement | Current console | Needed for licensed cash operation |
|---|---|---|
| Real-money funding | None; virtual credits only | Licensed payment/cash process, bank accounts, reconciliation |
| Cash-in / cash-out | Deliberately absent | Regulated cashier workflow, receipts, dual control, vault/transport |
| KYC and age checks | Staff accounts only; no player accounts | Player identity verification, age gating, sanctions screening |
| AML/CFT monitoring | None | Risk scoring, thresholds, alerts, STR/SAR filing |
| Player limits and self-exclusion | None | Deposit/loss/session limits, self-exclusion, cooling-off |
| Gaming licence disclosure | Play-money notice only | Licence number, operator identity, regulator contact |
| Independent fairness certification | None | Certified rules, RNG/payout testing, result audit |
| Regulatory reporting | Internal reports/snapshots only | Regulator-format event, player and financial reporting |
| Physical cash reconciliation | No physical cash | Shift counts, blind drops, variance investigation, secure transport |
| Dispute resolution | Internal lookup/history | Formal player complaints process and regulator escalation |
| Tax and withholding | None | Gaming tax, withholding and reporting |
| Data protection | Basic app controls | Formal privacy programme, DPIAs, retention and breach process |

**Do not connect physical cash to this console or describe it as real-money
betting until every applicable item above is closed and signed off by counsel
and the regulator.**

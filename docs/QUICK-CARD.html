E-SABONG CONSOLE
Quick card · POS teller & Game Master · keep at the counter
Every balance is a virtual credit with no cash value. No cash-in, cash-out, e-wallet or real-money payout is allowed.

POS teller

Start of shift

  1. Sign in with your own username and password.
  2. If this terminal is assigned to you, the shift opens automatically and the banner says “Clocked in on POS-…”. Otherwise press Open shift and add a note.
  3. Check Credits available and the Workstation code before taking money.
  4. No open shift = bet buttons disabled. That is normal, not an error.

Taking a bet

  1. Confirm the fight number on screen is the fight in progress.
  2. Type the amount — TAB jumps to the amount box.
  3. Choose the side: F2 MERON, F3 WALA, F4 DRAW.
  4. Wait for “Bet accepted” and ticket number. The receipt prints automatically.
  5. If it says duplicate submission: wait 5 seconds and retry once. Never retry a bet that already printed.

Paying a winning slip

  1. Scan the ticket, or press F9 and type the MANUAL CODE under the barcode.
  2. Check the screen says Pay out N credits and the amount matches the ticket.
  3. Hand over the credits, then press Record payout so it leaves the queue.
  4. “Already paid out” — never pay it again. “Not settled yet” — never pay it.
  5. Another terminal’s slip: send the player to that counter. Altered code: do not pay, call the Game Master.

End of shift

  1. All bets must be settled. If any are active, ask the Game Master to settle or void them.
  2. Press Close shift with a note. The shift report is sealed automatically.
  3. Print the closeout slip, sign it, hand it to the Game Master.
  4. Signing out on your assigned terminal closes the shift too — but only when nothing is active.

Never

  • Share a login or let anyone bet on your account.
  • Accept a bet without a receipt, “on the side”.
  • Pay an unsettled, altered or other-terminal slip.

Game Master

Run a fight

  1. Fights → Console: check the fight number, both corners and the odds.
  2. Open betting (only from scheduled). Teller screens show the fight within a few seconds — the cards slide in without reloading.
  3. Watch the live stakes. Use Meron Locked / Wala Locked if one side runs too heavy.
  4. Lock betting before the fight starts — no new bets after this.
  5. Mark live when the fight starts.
  6. Declare result: Meron pays red, Wala pays blue. Draw and Cancelled refund every stake. The button shows the amount it will move before you press it.
  7. Next fight after grading. Corners carry over; odds are pre-filled.

Rules that protect the floor

  • One fight at a time. A new fight cannot be created until the previous one has a declared winner.
  • Fight numbers must climb. Do not grade #3 before #2.
  • A result is terminal — never settle the same fight twice. Schedule a new fight instead.
  • Stop ratio caps the payout at stake × ratio, even when the posted odds are higher.
  • Regrade is for a wrong call, and is blocked once any winning slip has been paid out.
  • Cancelled needs a written reason and refunds all stakes.

Tellers & terminals

  • Create teller accounts and allocate virtual credits; a suspended teller cannot sign in.
  • POS monitor: bind a browser to a workstation, then ask the Supervisor to approve it.
  • Force-close shift needs a reason and seals a snapshot — unsettled slips stay visible.
  • Void an individual bet only with a reason.
  • Terminal assignment is done by the Supervisor in Accounts; one terminal belongs to one teller.
Before grading: confirm you are on the right fight number. A graded slip starts paying out immediately at the counters.
Fight card looks stale? It refreshes itself every 4 seconds. If it does not, check the LAN cable/server before touching anything.
Stuck mid-event: stop and call the Supervisor. Do not reboot the server, clear the browser, or sign in as another user. Supervisor only: credits, terminal approval, password resets, force-close, reconciliation and sealed reports.
